### 📌 Business Problem
BMW Board needs to answer:
1. What drives €1.57T revenue? (Region, Model, Year)
2. Is EV strategy working? (Electric Vehicle Adoption Trend)
3. How does fuel price affect units sold?
4. Which model is most profitable vs most sold?

### 📂 Dataset Overview
- **Period:** 2018-2025 (8 years)
- **Total Units Sold:** 24.5 Million
- **Total Revenue:** €1.57 Trillion (€1,570,000,000,000)
- **Top Model by Units:** iX (3.36M units)
- **Top Region by Revenue:** CHINA (~€401B)
- **Models:** 3 Series, 5 Series, i4, iX, MINI, X3, X5, X7
- **Regions:** China, Europe, RestOfWorld, USA

### 📈 Key Insights & Results

**KPIs:**
- Units Sold: 24.5M
- Total Revenue: €1.57T
- Top Model: iX
- Top Region: CHINA

**1. Region by Revenue - China Dominates:**
- China: **~€401,000,000,000 (€401B)** - Highest
- RestOfWorld: ~€389B
- USA: ~€387B
- Europe: ~€386B
> **Insight:** China generates €14B more than Europe despite being single country - 25.5% of total €1.57T. China strategy is working but risky.

**2. Yearly Trend of Revenue (2018-2025) - Steady Growth Despite Dips:**
- 2018: €176,775,218,399 (~€176.7B)
- 2019: €195,638,067,880 (~€195.6B) [+10.6% YoY]
- 2020: €197,101,324,263 (~€197B) [COVID resilience]
- 2021: €194,292,936,404 (~€194B) [Chip shortage dip]
- 2022: €203,582,572,723 (~€203B)
- 2023: €211,471,067,850 (~€211B)
- 2024: €215,605,681,914 (~€215B)
- 2025 Forecast: Growth continues
> **Insight:** Revenue grew 21.9% from 2018 (€176B) to 2025 (€215B) despite 2021 dip. CAGR ~2.8% - stable automotive growth.

**3. Model by Units Sold - iX Leads EV Revolution:**
1. iX: **3,369,912 units (13.7% share)**
2. i4: 3,125,687 units
3. X7: 3,112,074 units
4. X5: 3,085,134 units
5. 5 Series: 3,052,524 units
6. X3: 3,025,861 units
7. 3 Series: 3,006,048 units
8. MINI: 2,971,205 units
> **Insight:** Top 2 models by units are EVs (iX, i4) = 6.49M units = 26.5% of total. EV is not future, it's present. Even distribution - no model below 2.9M shows strong portfolio.

**4. Electric Vehicle Adoption Trend (2018-2025) - Linear Explosion:**
- 2018: ~7%
- 2020: ~25%
- 2022: ~45%
- 2024: ~65%
- 2025: **~74%**
> **Insight:** EV adoption grew 10x in 7 years from 7% to 74% - 8.5% annual increase. By 2025, 3 out of 4 BMWs sold are electric/hybrid. Validates iX/i4 investment.

**5. Model by Revenue - X7 is Most Profitable Despite Not Top in Units:**
- X7: **€286,427,856,714 (€286B) - Highest revenue**
- iX: €235,060,013,898 (€235B)
- X5: €212,814,244,306 (€212B)
- i4: €202,904,898,753 (€202B)
- 5 Series: €188,992,092,821 (€188B)
- X3: €175,547,140,738 (€175B)
- 3 Series: €144,338,948,833 (€144B)
- MINI: €124,934,102,065 (€124B) - Lowest
> **Insight:** X7 generates €286B with only 3.11M units = €92K per unit. MINI generates €124B with 2.97M units = €42K per unit. X7 revenue per unit is 2.19x MINI. Luxury SUV = profit king.

**6. Fuel Price Index vs Units Sold - Sweet Spot Identified:**
- 1.22-1.32: **~6.5M units (Peak)** - Best fuel price for sales
- 1.12-1.22: ~6.0M units
- 1.02-1.12: ~5.5M units
- 1.32-1.42: ~1.9M units - High fuel price kills sales
- 0.92-1.02: ~1.9M units - Too cheap = economic downturn?
> **Insight:** Moderate fuel price (1.12-1.32) drives 12.5M units = 51% of sales. Extremes (very high >1.32 or very low <1.02) collapse to 1.9M each. Pricing strategy should hedge fuel.

### 💡 Strategic Recommendations for BMW Board

1.  **Protect China:** €401B region - diversify supply chain, local EV production to avoid geopolitical risk.
2.  **Double Down on X7:** Highest revenue (€286B) + high per-unit value (€92K) - increase X7 marketing vs MINI.
3.  **EV is Winning:** iX top units (3.36M) proves EV adoption 7%→74% - accelerate i4/iX production, phase out low-margin MINI.
4.  **Fuel Price Hedging:** Sales peak at fuel index 1.22-1.32 - offer fuel-inclusive packages when fuel >1.32 to maintain sales.
5.  **Portfolio Balance:** All models 2.9M-3.3M units = healthy, but revenue gap €124B-€286B shows need to push customers from 3 Series/MINI to X7/X5.
